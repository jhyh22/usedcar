Verified Korean seller asking prices will be displayed in KRW without added markup. Freight and other charges are quoted separately. A seller asking price is not a confirmed final purchase cost.

One container is planned, subject to a professional loading assessment. A 40ft High Cube and a specialist rack may be needed; fit and safe securing have not yet been confirmed.
